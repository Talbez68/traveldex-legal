# TravelDex Support

TravelDex is an iPhone travel planner for the trip itself: nearby alerts for your saved places, day plans with walking routes, and a record of where you've been.

Keep kosher or eat vegan? See [Kosher and vegan food on your trip](kosher): how TravelDex shows who certifies a place.

## Contact
Questions, problems or ideas: **[traveldexsupport@gmail.com](mailto:traveldexsupport@gmail.com)**. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

## Common questions
**Nearby alerts don't arrive.** Settings → TravelDex → Location should be set to *Always*, and Notifications allowed. Check that alerts are on for the trip (trip → Alerts).

**Subscriptions.** TravelDex Pro is billed by Apple. To cancel or change it: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. To restore it on a new device: TravelDex → Settings → Restore purchases.

**Where is my data?** On your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if iCloud is on. We don't run servers that receive it.
