# TravelDex Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11 October 2026_

TravelDex is a travel planner. We (the developer) do not run servers that receive your data, do not use analytics or advertising, and do not track you. The only exception is feedback you choose to send from a test version (see "Test versions" below).

## Where your data is stored
Your trips, places, photos you add, packing list, preferences and badges are stored on your iPhone (and your Apple Watch, if you use the TravelDex Watch app). If iCloud is turned on, Apple stores a copy in your private iCloud account so your devices stay in sync. We cannot see it.

## Sharing a trip
If you share a trip, the people you invite see and can edit that trip's name, dates, places, plans, notes, visits, flights and tips through Apple's iCloud sharing. If the trip is part of a group of trips, they also see the group's name and the invitation links of its other trips. Sharing a whole group (Share group) invites them to every trip in it, using the same people and permissions. Your photos, preferences, badges, packing list and alert settings are never shared. You can stop sharing at any time from the trip's Share trip screen, or the group's Share group screen.

## Permissions
- **Location** – to show distances, order walking routes and alert you when you're near a saved place (with "Always", also when the app is closed). Your location is not sent to us.
- **Photos** – to add photos you took at your saved places (and, if you choose, photos from your shared albums), suggest which places you visited, find past trips, and read places or tips from screenshots and videos you pick. Photos are analysed on your device.
- **Camera** – to photograph the things you pack.
- **Speech recognition** – to turn what's said in a travel video you choose into tips. The speech is recognised on your iPhone.
- **Calendar** – to import bookings for your trip days and add the stops and flights you schedule to your calendar.
- **Notifications** – for nearby alerts, arrival questions and check-out reminders.

Text recognition in photos and screenshots, place labels, tips from videos and packing suggestions use Apple's on-device features (including Apple Intelligence when available). Nothing is uploaded for them.

## Services the app contacts
To look up places and plan your days, TravelDex sends the place's name, a link you pasted, or coordinates — and nothing that identifies you — to:
- Apple Maps (search, details, routes, travel times, Look Around)
- Apple Weather (weather for your trip days: only rounded coordinates of where the day's stops are, and the dates)
- OpenStreetMap servers (opening hours, cuisine, dietary tags)
- Wikimedia Commons (place photos)
- the place's own website, when it has one (opening hours, menus, dietary information, photos)
- Google Maps, only when you paste or share a Google Maps link (to open the link and read its places)
- TikTok, only when you paste or share a TikTok link (to read the video's caption)
- Google Places, in test versions only (photos, opening hours)

These services have their own privacy policies. They only receive what's needed to answer each request.

## Test versions
In TestFlight test versions you can send feedback by shaking the phone or from Settings → Send feedback. Only when you tap Send, we receive what you wrote, a screenshot of the screen you were on, the app version, your device model, iOS version and language, and, if you include it, the app's arrival log (recent nearby-alert decisions with place names and distances). It is stored in TravelDex's iCloud database, read only by the developer to fix problems, and deleted when no longer needed. The App Store version has no feedback button and sends nothing.

## Purchases
Subscriptions and Trip Passes are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

## Children
TravelDex is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your data
Delete a trip in the app, or delete the app. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → TravelDex. To have test-version feedback you sent deleted, email us.

## Changes
When TravelDex starts using your data in a new way, this page is updated first, with a new date above.
