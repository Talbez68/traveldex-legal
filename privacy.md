# TravelDex Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 October 2026_

TravelDex is a travel planner. We (the developer) do not run servers that receive your data, do not use analytics or advertising, and do not track you.

## Where your data is stored
Your trips, places, photos you add, preferences and badges are stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is turned on, Apple stores a copy in your private iCloud account so your devices stay in sync. We cannot see it.

## Permissions
- **Location** – to show distances, order walking routes and alert you when you're near a saved place (with "Always", also when the app is closed). Your location is not sent to us.
- **Photos** – to add photos you took at your saved places, suggest which places you visited, and find past trips. Photos are analysed on your device.
- **Calendar** – to import bookings for your trip days and add the stops you schedule to your calendar.
- **Notifications** – for nearby alerts.

## Services the app contacts
To look up places, TravelDex sends the place's name or coordinates, and nothing that identifies you, to:
- Apple Maps (search, details, routes, Look Around)
- OpenStreetMap servers (opening hours, cuisine, dietary tags)
- Wikimedia Commons (place photos)
- the place's own website, when it has one (menus, dietary information, photos)

These services have their own privacy policies. They only receive what's needed to answer each request.

## Purchases
Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

## Children
TravelDex is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your data
Delete a trip in the app, or delete the app. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → TravelDex.
