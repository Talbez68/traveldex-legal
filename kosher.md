# Kosher and vegan food on your trip

When you keep kosher, eat vegan or need halal or gluten-free food, a trip planner that just says "kosher" isn't enough. You want to know **who says so**. TravelDex shows you the evidence for every dietary label, and says plainly when there isn't any.

## What TravelDex checks
For every restaurant, café and bakery you save, TravelDex looks for real evidence:

- **The place's own website.** It reads the homepage and up to three pages it links to (kosher certificate, FAQ, about, menu) for clear statements such as a named kosher agency or "100% vegan".
- **The kosher certificate itself.** When the site posts its certificate as an image or PDF, TravelDex reads it on your iPhone, showing the agency, meat/dairy and the expiry date. A certificate that has expired is marked "not confirmed".
- **OpenStreetMap.** Dietary tags added by local mappers.
- **You.** Your own labels always win.

## What you see
- **Kosher**, with the evidence underneath, for example *Their certificate: "International Kosher Council (IKC) · Meat"*.
- **Kosher?** when the place only claims it without naming a supervising agency (a slogan, a search keyword, a social media category).
- **Not kosher** when the site says it isn't under supervision, calls itself "kosher-style", or its menu has pork or shellfish dishes.
- **Not known to be kosher** when nothing was found either way. TravelDex never guesses.

The same goes for vegan, vegetarian, halal and gluten-free. Words like "kosher salt", "kosher-style" or "vegan options on request" don't count as proof.

## Set it as a must-have
In Preferences, set a diet as a **must-have**. TravelDex then:
- warns you about saved places that aren't known to meet it, and can hide them,
- leaves those places out when it suggests stops for a day,
- searches for places that do ("Kosher ramen") when it suggests new ones, marked *not confirmed* until there's evidence,
- never sends a nearby alert for a food place you've hidden.

## Private by design
All of this runs on your iPhone. Certificates and photos are read on the device, and there's no account and no tracking. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).

TravelDex shows what it found and where it found it. It can't vouch for a kitchen, so when it matters, check with the restaurant or the agency.
